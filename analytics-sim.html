HOW CLOSE WAS EACH TITLE RUN, REALLY?
Before you look at your number: this page does not question whether anyone's championship was real or earned. It measures how CLOSE the actual playoff games were, by replaying each champion's real bracket 10,000 times using their own real scoring ability. A low percentage means the games were genuinely tight - close enough that normal variance could plausibly have flipped a result - not that the title was somehow fake. Full methodology below.
