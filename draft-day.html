LEGEND — WHAT AM I LOOKING AT?
Legendary — elite value score (1,800+). A very late pick that massively outproduced its draft slot.
Rare — strong value score (1,000–1,800). Solid production relative to where they were drafted.
Common — moderate value score (400–1,000). Roughly expected output for the slot.
Junk — low value score (under 400). Usually an early pick that underproduced badly.
Note: QBs and Stars are excluded from the four rarity tiers above and get their own sections instead. QBs are typically drafted late but score heavily, and round-1 picks can't benefit from value score's round multiplier at all - both would be misleadingly under-colored by this system. See FAQ below.
